Our approach

Nothing about your house is a surprise.

We walk every client through the same six stages we just walked you through: survey, foundation, frame, envelope, grounds, reveal. You get photos at each one, not just at the end.

That means you know what's in the ground before it's covered, what's in the walls before they're closed, and what's on the invoice before it's due. Custom estates, built in the open, on a schedule you can actually read.

01 – 06

The build, in order

Every Strata home moves through the same six stages, on the same site, in view of the same client. Nothing is out of sequence.

01
0:00 – 0:01
Site & Survey
We stake the pad, run utilities, and confirm every setback against the plat before a single yard of earth moves.
02
0:01 – 0:03
Foundation
Forms go in, footings are inspected, and the slab cures on its own schedule — no shortcuts on what you won't see later.
03
0:03 – 0:04
Frame
The house gets its skeleton — walls, trusses, roofline — sequenced so mechanicals and windows land exactly where the drawings say.
04
0:04 – 0:05
Envelope
Stone, stucco, and roofing close the house in and make it weathertight, inside and out, before finishes ever start.
05
0:05 – 0:07
Grounds
Drives, drainage, and planting are graded to the same tolerances as the foundation. The yard is engineered too.
06
0:07 – 0:08
Reveal
Final walkthrough, punch list, keys. You move into a house you've already watched built, stage by stage.
